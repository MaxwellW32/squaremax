# Squaremax Marketing Plan

How to get the first 100 paying businesses onto Squaremax Sites, in order of
return on effort, written for a one-person shop in Jamaica with a small budget.
Written 2026-09-04. Revisit after the first 25 clients.

The product sells one promise: **"Your business online tonight — bookings and
orders included — from US$10/month, edit it yourself."** Every channel below
just puts that sentence in front of the right person.

---

## 0. Before spending a dollar (this week)

1. **Two demo sites that look like real Kingston businesses.** `/fade-district`
   and `/pepper-and-thyme` are seeded. Replace their placeholder photos with real
   uploads (the editor now has Upload) so screenshots look like a real barber and
   a real kitchen. Every ad and post links to one of them.
2. **Your own site is the proof.** squaremaxtech.com must load fast on a phone
   on Digicel data. Check with Google PageSpeed on the home page and a demo.
3. **Measurement on.** Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID` (GA4) and
   `NEXT_PUBLIC_META_PIXEL_ID` in production. The site already fires a
   `purchase` conversion on the go-live page, which is what Google and Meta
   optimise toward. Without this, ads are guessing.
4. **Google Business Profile for Squaremax** (Kingston). Free, ranks for
   "website designer Kingston" within weeks, collects reviews.
5. **WhatsApp Business number** for Squaremax with a catalog entry per plan
   (Website $10 / Service $15 / Storefront $20) and quick replies. Jamaican SMBs
   buy over WhatsApp, not email.

---

## 1. Founding-member offer (month 1–2)

You have zero paying clients, so the first 20 are worth more as proof than as
revenue. Run a **Founding 20** offer:

- Concierge setup free (normally US$99): "send me your photos, menu and hours on
  WhatsApp and I'll build it tonight."
- First month on us when they prepay 3 months (you bank the cash, they get skin
  in the game).
- Ask for two things in return: a Google review of Squaremax and permission to
  screenshot their site.

Where to find the 20: businesses you already know, the places you already buy
from, and every barber/salon/food spot with an Instagram bio that says "DM to
book" or "WhatsApp to order." That phrase is your entire target market.

Script (DM or in person): *"I build websites for small businesses here — you
get online booking (or orders) built in for US$15 a month, and you can change
anything yourself from your phone. Here's one I did for a barber: [link]. I'm
setting up 20 businesses free this month, want yours done tonight?"*

## 2. Channels, ranked

### A. Instagram + TikTok (organic) — your primary channel, US$0

Your buyers live on Instagram. The content that works for this product is
**screen recordings**, not graphics:

- "Watch me build a barbershop site in 4 minutes" (time-lapse of the wizard,
  phone in hand, end on the QR code).
- "Customer books at 11pm, you wake up to this" (the booking email + dashboard).
- "Order comes in → tap Mark paid → stock updates" (the Orders tab).
- Before/after: their Instagram bio "DM to book" → their new booking page.
- One post per client launch: "Fade District is live 🎉" tagging them (they
  reshare = free reach into their customers, who are also business owners).

Cadence: 3 posts + daily stories. Use the Reels format everywhere; TikTok gets
the same video. Hashtags: #jamaicabusiness #kingstonjamaica #jamaicanbusiness
#smallbusinessjamaica #876 plus the trade (#jamaicabarber #jamaicasalon
#jamaicanfood).

### B. Meta ads (Instagram/Facebook) — first paid channel, US$5–10/day

Meta is where Jamaican SMB owners are, and targeting is good enough to reach
"people in Jamaica who run a Facebook Page" cheaply.

- **Objective:** Leads → landing on `/pricing` or `/sites/start` (conversion
  = `purchase` event, already wired). Until you have ~20 purchases, optimise for
  "landing page views" instead so the algorithm has signal.
- **Audience:** Jamaica, 25–55, interests: small business, entrepreneurship,
  Instagram for business, barbershop / beauty salon / restaurant owners;
  behaviour "Facebook Page admins." Later: Lookalike of your customer list.
- **Creative:** the same screen-recording Reels. Test 3 hooks:
  1. "Still taking bookings by DM? Here's a website that does it for you."
  2. "US$10 a month for a real website. Built in Jamaica."
  3. "Your customers can order from your site tonight. No app, no commission."
- **Budget:** US$150/month to start. Kill anything above US$15 cost-per-lead
  after 2 weeks; double what's under US$8.
- **Retargeting:** people who visited `/sites/start` but didn't pay, for 14
  days, with the founding offer.

### C. Google Ads (Search) — small and precise, US$3–5/day

Search volume in Jamaica is low, but intent is perfect and competition is thin.

- Campaign 1 (exact/phrase): "website designer jamaica", "website design
  kingston", "cheap website jamaica", "make a website for my business jamaica",
  "booking website jamaica", "online store jamaica".
- Campaign 2: competitor terms — "wix jamaica", "squarespace jamaica",
  "booksy jamaica", "fresha jamaica".
- Landing page: `/pricing` for price queries, `/` for the rest.
- Negatives: "free", "job", "course", "tutorial".
- Expect ~US$0.30–1.00/click. US$100/month is plenty to learn.

### D. Google Search (SEO) — compounding, free

- The sitemap now includes every live client site, so each client is a
  backlink-free ranking page for their own name. Encourage clients to put their
  squaremaxtech.com link on their Google Business Profile.
- Write three plain pages, one per trade, and link them from the home page:
  `/for/barbers`, `/for/restaurants`, `/for/salons` — "A website for barbers in
  Jamaica with online booking, US$15/month." Each shows the relevant demo and
  plan. (Not built yet; ~1 hour each with the existing components.)
- One blog post a month answering a real question: "How much does a website
  cost in Jamaica in 2026?", "Booksy vs your own booking page", "How to take
  orders on WhatsApp without losing track." These rank in Jamaica within weeks.

### E. WhatsApp + referrals — highest conversion, free

- **Referral reward:** any client who refers a business that goes live gets a
  free month. It costs you US$10–20 and replaces an ad spend of US$30+.
- Put a "Refer a business" line in the renewal-reminder email (already sent
  before each period ends) and in the dashboard Overview.
- Ask every client for a 20-second phone video: "I set this up in one evening."
  Post it. Nothing sells like a peer.

### F. In person — Kingston is small

- Print the Squaremax QR (links to `/pricing`) on a business card; leave it at
  every barber, salon and cookshop you visit. The pitch is the demo on your
  phone; the QR is how they find you later.
- One evening at a JBDC / Chamber / church business group: 10-minute demo,
  founding offer, sign-ups on the spot from your phone.
- Partner with someone who already talks to SMBs every day: an accountant, a
  Digicel Business rep, a photographer. 20% of first-year revenue per referral.

### G. Custom Builds (the studio) — different buyer, different channel

Custom Builds clients are companies with US$1,000–6,500 budgets: they come via
referrals, LinkedIn, and Upwork/Fiverr-style profiles, not Instagram Reels. Keep
a portfolio page current, post one case study a month on LinkedIn, and let the
Sites product be the top of the funnel (a Sites client who outgrows the template
becomes a Custom Build).

## 3. Landing page and funnel notes

- Home page hero now leads with the outcome ("online tonight"), the price
  (US$10) and "built in Jamaica"; plans are three cards with the Service bundle
  marked most popular. Send ads to `/pricing` when the ad mentions price, to `/`
  otherwise.
- The wizard preselects a bundle from `?plan=service` / `?plan=storefront`, so
  ad links can deep-link straight into checkout intent:
  `squaremaxtech.com/sites/start?plan=service`.
- Sign-in is email magic links; no Google account needed. The wizard carries
  the typed business name through sign-in.
- Trust gap to close as soon as possible: real client logos/screenshots in a
  "Businesses on Squaremax" strip on the home page, and 3 real testimonials.
  Don't fabricate — use the founding 20.

## 4. Numbers to watch (weekly, 10 minutes)

| Metric | Where | Healthy |
|---|---|---|
| Visits → `/sites/start` | GA4 | > 15% of visits |
| Start → paid | admin page tenants vs GA4 | > 20% of starts |
| Cost per paying client (ads) | Meta/Google spend ÷ purchases | < US$40 (≈ 3 months of revenue) |
| Renewal rate | admin page: active ÷ (active + suspended) | > 85% |
| Referrals per client | ask at renewal | > 0.3 |

At 100 clients averaging US$14/month you are at US$1,400/month recurring on
under US$50/month infrastructure; the goal of this plan is to get there inside
six months mostly on organic + referrals, with ads as the accelerator.

---

## 5. Not worth it yet

- Facebook groups spam, cold email, and marketplaces (Fiverr) for the Sites
  product — wrong buyer, wrong price point.
- A "free plan." It attracts people who never pay and multiplies support.
  Free concierge setup for the founding 20 is the better version of free.
- Radio/print. Fine for awareness, impossible to measure at this size.
